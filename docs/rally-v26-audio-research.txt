V2.6 audio research — 11 October 2026

Free source licences and author attribution are in public/rally/audio-credits.txt.
The rally and kart recordings were evaluated for steady spectral regions and
clean looping; subjective listening on actual iPhone speakers remains a user
acceptance check. Four tonal rally variants share one real interior recording;
the kart has a separate venue recording. Do not advertise exact model authenticity.

Co-driver follow-up:
PacenotePal (https://github.com/Koenvh1/PacenotePal) documents useful phrase-bank
and route-trigger architecture. Its linked voice packs include voices extracted
from other games. A mod download or source-code licence is not permission to
redistribute the recordings in Pocket Rally. No freely licensed English recording
bank with clear redistribution permission was verified. Per the approved fallback,
voice playback is not shipped and no unavailable toggle is exposed.

Implementation once an original/appropriately licensed voice bank is available:
Prepare English left/right 1–6 (1 tightest, 6 fastest), hairpin, into, crest and
finish phrases offline; band-limit around 350–3000Hz, compress lightly, retain
intelligibility. Author per-route forward/reverse notes and review in driving tests.
Schedule calls 2–3 seconds before each bend based on route distance and speed,
never nearest-point jumps; one voice source, no repeated calls, pause/resume and
recovery resynchronisation, subtitles and remembered toggle. Kart mode has no
co-driver. No live speech generation or network requests while driving.

V2.6.1 update — 11 October 2026
Original co-driver recording verified: SentientMattress, Rally Co-Driver Pacenote

Conventional trimming/EQ only; no generative processing. See audio credits.
27 preloaded clips, 24 explicit route-direction note lists, speed-based lead,
subtitles and remembered toggle now implemented. The earlier deferral is resolved.

Verification: 46 regression tests; correct left/right geometry for every authored
anchor, scheduling/deduplication/recovery, unavailable voice assets, bounded voice
sources and cached decode, plus existing leaderboard/replay/route checks.
Browser checks cover desktop, portrait phone, landscape phone and tablet layouts,
including a temporary ghost/subtitle fixture (removed before release).
No additional draw calls from this update. Playback uses a bounded raycast against
the existing road mesh; audio adds 27 short cached buffers and at most two scheduled
non-overlapping voice sources. Physical iPhone performance/audio and PS5 rumble
remain unverified; browser viewport simulation does not validate device hardware.
