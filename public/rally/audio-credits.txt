The author identifies the source as a public-domain car recording and
confirms the loops were remade from that source in the page comments.

Pocket Rally modifications: mono resampling, loop-boundary crossfade,
DC removal, tonal EQ, saturation, pitch and level adjustment. Eight
idle/load variants are derived from one recording. These are stylised
car voices, not recordings of the named Subaru, Ford, Toyota or Lancia.

The source recording is preserved in scripts/audio-source/car-engine-cc0.wav.
Rebuild with python3 scripts/generate-rally-audio.py.
