Pocket Rally V2.5 design references

The cars are original chunky clay-style approximations, not licensed manufacturer models.
The four additional period-inspired cars are:
Ford Sierra RS Cosworth (1990), Volkswagen Golf GTI 16V (1990),
Peugeot 205 GTI (1990) and Renault Clio Williams (1993).
