Durham Pixels · Pocket Racers

Privacy policy

This policy covers the first private Alpha of Pocket Racers for Meta Quest.

Last updated 25 October 2026

Who is responsible?

Pocket Racers is developed by Mark Whitley, working under the name Durham Pixels. Mark Whitley is responsible for the personal information described here. Durham Pixels is a developer name, not a registered company.

For support, privacy questions or a request about your information, email durhampixels@gmail.com.

A short explanation for players and parents

The first Alpha is a local racing game. Driver names, race results and settings stay on the headset; they are not published to an online leaderboard. Use a nickname rather than your real name. There is no player chat, advertising or in-game purchase in this Alpha.

Meta accounts must meet Meta’s age requirements. Children aged 10–12 need a parent-managed account where supported. The game does not make an under-10 account eligible to use Quest.

Information used by the game

The game stores driver nicknames, local race results and preferences on your device so you can keep your settings and compare times on that headset. We do not receive these local records.

The Alpha uses Meta’s platform services to check access to the game and request an age category, such as child, teen or adult. It does not request a date of birth or retrieve a Meta profile name or photograph. The age category is not saved by the game. All Alpha players receive the same local-only experience. We use this limited information for our legitimate interests in providing the game, preserving local progress, preventing unauthorised access and supporting an age-appropriate experience; we do not use it for marketing or profiling.

Headset and controller tracking are used to display and control the game. We do not receive recordings of your room or your movements from the game. Development diagnostics may store technical performance measurements locally on the headset; they are not automatically sent to us. Meta and Unity may process their own platform or engine diagnostics under their applicable privacy policies, including Unity’s player privacy policy.

If you contact us

We receive your email address, message and any files you choose to send. Please avoid sending children’s names, account details or images unless necessary to explain a problem. We use correspondence to answer questions, investigate problems and handle privacy requests. Our basis for this is our legitimate interest in supporting players and protecting their privacy.

We keep correspondence only as long as needed to resolve the enquiry and any related follow-up, dispute or legal requirement. Local game records remain on the device until removed; any platform backups are controlled through Meta’s services.

Other services

Meta operates the headset, account, Store and platform services under its own privacy policy. Email sent to our Gmail address is handled by Google. Google handles email under its privacy policy. This policy page is hosted by Vercel on markwhitley.design. Visiting it sends ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and browser request, to the hosting service to deliver and secure the page. We have not added advertising, tracking scripts or cookies to this page. See Vercel’s privacy policy for its processing. Provider services may process information outside the UK, as described in their policies. Contact us for information about the services used for your enquiry.

We do not sell personal information or operate an advertising profile or public leaderboard in this Alpha.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us for access to information we hold about you, correction, deletion or restriction, and data portability where applicable. A parent or guardian can contact us about their child’s information. We may need proportionate information to confirm the request is authorised. We cannot remotely read or delete records stored only on your headset; contact us for help removing local game data.

You can object to our use of information based on legitimate interests. Send your request to durhampixels@gmail.com.

You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Changes

We will update this policy before introducing features that change how information is used. This policy is available at markwhitley.design/pocket-racers/privacy.html. If it moves to a Durham Pixels website, we will update the app’s listing and maintain a redirect where possible.
